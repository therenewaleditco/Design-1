# Daily Rituals (dailyrituals.uk) — Visual Style Reference

Source: homepage + blog post screenshots, dailyrituals.uk (UK wellness/self-care/biohacking blog for women), reviewed July 2026.

## 1. The Vibe

Polished-but-unfiltered big-sister energy. It reads like a trend-obsessed friend's group chat that got a magazine layout: candid bathroom-mirror selfies and grainy phone shots sit next to classic serif headlines and clean grid layouts. Editorial structure, TikTok/Pinterest moodboard content.

One-liner: **elevated girl-next-door meets glossy wellness mag.**

Usage pattern: the deep green is the one bold color in the whole system — it's the button fill, the footer/newsletter block background, the logo wordmark, and the background of Instagram quote-card graphics. Everything else is neutral (black/white/grey), so the green reads as "the brand color" every time it shows up.

## 3. Typography

Three-font system, each with a distinct job:

**Script/display (logo + emotional headlines):** an elegant, flowing calligraphic script. Used for the "Daily Rituals" wordmark, "Get your ✨Wellness Dose✨" banner copy, and headline text inside Instagram graphics. Closest free/Google-Fonts equivalents: *Beloved Script*, *Alex Brush*, *Herr Von Muellerhoff*, or *Windsong*.

**Editorial serif (article headlines, H2s):** a classic high-contrast serif with visible thick/thin strokes. Closest equivalents: *Playfair Display*, *Lora*, or *Libre Caslon Text*.

**Monospace (nav, category eyebrows, buttons):** uppercase, wide letter-spacing, fixed-width characters. Used for the main nav ("NEWSLETTER TRENDING WELLNESS SELF CARE RETREATS"), category tags above headlines ("SELF CARE", "TRENDING"), and CTA button labels ("READ MORE"). Closest equivalents: *Space Mono*, *IBM Plex Mono*, *Courier Prime*.

**Sans-serif (body copy, footer links):** clean, geometric, no-frills. Closest equivalents: *Jost*, *Poppins*, *Work Sans*.

Pairing rule of thumb: script for the one big emotional flourish, serif for headlines, mono for anything structural/UI, sans for reading copy.

## 4. Layout & UI Patterns

- Sharp corners everywhere. No rounded cards, no drop shadows. (The one exception: the homepage newsletter signup uses a rounded pill-shaped email field.)
- Full-bleed photography paired with generous white space — images are never boxed in with padding or borders.
- Featured articles: large image on one side, text block on the other (image often takes 55-60% of the width).
- List/grid sections ("Most Read", "Trending Now", "You'll Also Love"): 3-column card grid, image on top, category eyebrow + serif headline below, no card borders.
- Every headline gets a small uppercase mono category eyebrow directly above it (e.g. "WELLNESS", "TRENDING").
- Section headers get a small centered black underline/divider beneath them (see "You'll Also Love").
- CTA buttons: solid deep green fill, rectangular (sharp corners), white uppercase mono text, generous letter-spacing.
- Social icons: minimal line-icon glyphs inside thin circle outlines, black on white.
- Footer: deep green "Get on the List" block up top, then a plain white multi-column link footer (EXPLORE / INFO / ON THE BLOG), then a strip of Instagram thumbnails at the very bottom.

## 5. Photography & Image Style

This is the most distinctive part of the brand — do not swap in generic stock photography.

- **Grain is deliberate and constant.** Nearly every photo has a visible film-grain/noise texture layered on top. Nothing looks smooth or over-retouched.
- **Warm, high-contrast color grading.** Skin tones are pushed warm and rosy rather than neutral or cool.
- **Candid "get ready with me" energy.** Bathroom mirror selfies, hands mid-skincare-routine, half-finished vanity shots, phone visible in frame — never a posed studio portrait.
- **Collage/moodboard framing.** Many hero images layer 2-4 photos into a single frame (e.g. a mirror selfie + a product shot + a phone screen combined), rather than one clean single image.
- **Product flatlays are styled loosely** on fabric, tile, or pastel floral backdrops — not sterile white studio backgrounds.
- **Instagram graphics** break format entirely: solid deep-green background with a short punchy line of script + sans text overlaid in white (e.g. "be Addicted to real dopamine," "getting prepped for Hot Girl summer") — like a relatable meme/quote card, not a photo.

## 6. Brand Voice (for context, not the focus of this kit)

Tagline: *"Your dose of what's hot in wellness, self-care and biohacking."*
Tone from actual post copy: direct, funny, a little confessional ("I've suffered with my skin since I was a teen..."), name-drops trends and celebrities, uses "&" instead of "and," asks rhetorical questions to hook the reader, never hedges.
